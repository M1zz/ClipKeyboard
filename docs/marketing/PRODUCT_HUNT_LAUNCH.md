## First Comment (메이커 코멘트, 런칭 즉시 게시)

```
Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Leeo, a solo developer from Korea. I built ClipKeyboard because I kept
retyping the same things every day: my bank account number, my address,
"I'm in a meeting, will get back to you soon."

The turning point: I almost sent a client a bank account number with one
digit wrong. That evening I started building a keyboard that remembers
these things for me.

Today, ClipKeyboard is:

⌨️ A full keyboard in 23 languages with iPhone-style layouts, and your saved
   phrases on the same keyboard, so there is no switching back and forth
   (Japanese and Chinese type kana, Pinyin and Zhuyin for now; kanji
   conversion is on the roadmap)
🧠 A smart clipboard: copies are sorted by type (email, phone, address,
   IBAN with checksum validation, card numbers…)
📝 Templates with {variables}: "Hi {name}, invoice {number} is due {date}"
🔗 Stacks: insert several values in sequence
🔒 Your snippets stay on your device (and your own iCloud if you turn on
   backup). No third-party analytics SDK.

Pricing: every feature is free for your first 100 inserts, and what you
made keeps working after that. Pro is a one-time purchase or a yearly
subscription.

I'd love your feedback: especially from freelancers and anyone doing
customer support from their phone. I'll be here all day answering questions!
```
